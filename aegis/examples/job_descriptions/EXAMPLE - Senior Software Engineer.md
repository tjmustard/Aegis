# Senior Software Engineer — Platform Infrastructure
**Acme Corp** | Remote (US) | Full-time

## About the Role

Acme Corp is looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join our Platform Infrastructure team. You'll design and build the systems that underpin our developer experience, owning projects from technical design through production.

## Responsibilities

- Architect and implement scalable backend services in Go and Python
- Lead cross-functional technical design reviews and mentor junior engineers
- Drive reliability initiatives: SLOs, alerting, and incident response
- Partner with product and design to translate ambiguous requirements into clear technical plans
- Contribute to team roadmap prioritization and quarterly planning

## Qualifications

**Required:**
- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Production experience with distributed systems and cloud infrastructure (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Strong fundamentals in algorithms, data structures, and system design
- Experience with CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure-as-code (Terraform, Pulumi)
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills

**Nice to Have:**
- Experience with Kubernetes and container orchestration at scale
- Background in developer productivity or platform engineering
- Open-source contributions or public technical writing

## About Acme Corp

Acme Corp builds tools that help engineering teams ship faster. Founded in 2018, we're a Series B company with 150 employees and a strong remote-first culture.

**Compensation:** $175,000–$215,000 base + equity + benefits
**Location:** Remote (US timezones preferred)
